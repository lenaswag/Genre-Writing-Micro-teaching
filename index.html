1Claim주장 · tells our opinion
YOUR TASKFill in the blanks on your worksheet with your group's position (Agree / Disagree).

Q1What does a claim do?

It tells our opinion.

Q2Which expressions can we use to give an opinion?

In my opinion,I believe thatI think that
Disagree? → I don't believe thatI don't think that

Q3Which modal verb can we use to make a claim?

should + base verb → Schools should prioritize green spaces.
✗ should prioritizes
Example · Lesson 1

I believe that schools should prioritize green, nature-friendly spaces over high-tech indoor facilities.

워크시트의 Claim은 빈칸이에요. Agree 모둠과 Disagree 모둠이 각자 입장에 맞게 채워요. 정답 문장은 보여주지 않고 표현과 비교 구조만 짚어요.

Q1What does a reason do? And evidence?

찬성 AGREE
Pair 1
REASONDigital textbooks can reduce paper use.
EVIDENCEFor example, one tablet can hold all of a student's textbooks, so schools do not need to print thousands of paper books.
Pair 2
REASONPaper textbooks create a lot of waste every year.
EVIDENCEFor instance, many students throw away their old textbooks at the end of the school year.
반대 DISAGREE
Pair 1
REASONDigital textbooks use a lot of energy.
EVIDENCEFor example, students charge their tablets almost every day, and much electricity comes from burning coal.
Pair 2
REASONDigital devices can create harmful waste.
EVIDENCEFor instance, old tablets contain materials that can pollute the environment if they are not recycled properly.

수준별 지원: 잘하는 모둠은 힌트 카드 없이 스스로 만들도록 격려하고 why/how 질문("Why is this a good reason?" "How does your evidence show it?")을 해요. 도움이 필요한 모둠에는 힌트 카드와 예/아니오·둘 중 고르기 질문("Is it about air or about paper?")을 써요.
4Conclusion결론 · says our claim again in different words
YOUR TASKWrite your claim again in different words on your worksheet.

Q1What does a conclusion do?

It says our claim again in different words.
Same opinion, different words.

Q2How can we start a conclusion?

In conclusion,To sum up,For these reasons,
Model · School Uniforms
CLAIMI believe that school uniforms are good for students.
↓
CONCLUSIONIn conclusion, school uniforms are a smart choice for students.
시범 중간에 "'good for students'… what's another way to say it?"라고 물어 학생이 바꿀 말을 직접 말하게 한 뒤 결론을 보여줘요.
